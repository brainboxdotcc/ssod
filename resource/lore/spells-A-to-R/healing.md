## Spell: Healing

The Healing spell allows the caster to restore vitality to another living creature, replenishing ten stamina points and helping the recipient recover from injury, exhaustion or physical hardship. Unlike spells that merely conceal pain or temporarily strengthen the body, Healing draws upon restorative magic to mend damage and renew the natural life force of its subject.

Throughout Cryptillia, Healing is amongst the most valued forms of magic, particularly by adventurers, healers and those who regularly face the dangers of the wilderness. A timely casting can mean the difference between life and death for an injured companion, making even a modestly skilled healer a welcome addition to any travelling party.

However, the spell has strict limitations. Its restorative power works only upon living creatures and cannot benefit the undead or demonic beings, whose unnatural existence lies beyond the reach of ordinary healing magic. Furthermore, the caster cannot direct the enchantment upon themselves, making Healing an act of assistance rather than self-preservation.

The spell also demands a considerable period of magical recovery, restricting its use to once per day. A sorcerer must therefore choose carefully when to expend this precious ability, particularly when travelling with companions whose injuries may be numerous and whose dangers are far from over. Healing can restore lost vitality, but it cannot guarantee survival against every wound or misfortune.

Amongst Cryptillian healers, an old saying serves as a reminder of both the spell's value and its limitations: *A healer may save another's life, but must trust another to save their own.*
