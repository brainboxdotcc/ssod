## Spell: Run

The Run spell allows the caster to move at twice their normal speed, granting a remarkable burst of agility and swiftness for a limited time. The enchantment enhances the caster's natural movement, allowing them to cover great distances, evade pursuers and react to danger with extraordinary speed. However, this magical acceleration comes at a price, as the body must eventually recover from the unnatural exertion.

Throughout Cryptillia, Run is particularly valued by adventurers, scouts, messengers and warriors who rely upon speed rather than brute strength. In combat, it allows the caster to outmanoeuvre opponents, avoid incoming attacks and strike before an enemy can react. Beyond the battlefield, the spell can prove invaluable when escaping dangerous creatures, crossing hazardous terrain or delivering an urgent message.

Unlike ordinary running, the spell temporarily pushes the caster beyond their natural physical limitations. Once its effects expire, a period of magical exhaustion follows, reducing the caster's speed to half its normal value until they have recovered. Repeated castings compound this fatigue, making reckless reliance upon the enchantment increasingly dangerous.

The greatest risk lies in misjudging when the spell will wear off. A sorcerer who confidently outruns a pursuing beast may suddenly discover that their legs can barely carry them, while the creature behind them has suffered no such inconvenience. Experienced spellcasters therefore learn to reserve sufficient time and distance for recovery, rather than exhausting themselves in the middle of an escape.

Amongst Cryptillian adventurers, a familiar warning is often repeated: *Running twice as fast is little comfort when you can barely walk afterwards.*
