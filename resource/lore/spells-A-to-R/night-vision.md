## Spell: Night Vision

The Night Vision spell allows the caster to see clearly in darkness without the need for torches, lanterns or other sources of illumination. By magically enhancing their sight, the enchantment reveals surroundings that would otherwise remain concealed in shadow. The clarity and effectiveness of this enhanced vision depend upon the caster's magical proficiency, with experienced sorcerers capable of navigating even the darkest environments with remarkable ease.

Throughout Cryptillia, Night Vision is particularly valued by adventurers, scouts, hunters and those who prefer to travel unseen. It provides much the same practical benefits as the Light spell, allowing the caster to explore underground passages, traverse unfamiliar terrain and detect dangers lurking beyond the reach of ordinary sight. Unlike Light, however, Night Vision produces no visible illumination, making it especially useful when discretion is more important than visibility.

The spell offers a considerable advantage when approaching unsuspecting enemies or avoiding creatures attracted to light. A sorcerer under its influence can move through darkness without announcing their presence with a conspicuous magical glow. However, the enchantment benefits only the caster's own vision, leaving companions to stumble blindly unless they possess similar abilities or carry their own sources of light.

Despite its usefulness, Night Vision does not grant the ability to see through solid objects, penetrate magical concealment or perceive dangers that are otherwise invisible. Darkness may no longer hinder the caster's sight, but hidden traps, concealed enemies and unexpected obstacles remain threats to the unwary.

Amongst Cryptillian scouts, a familiar saying serves as a reminder of the spell's greatest advantage: *The safest light is often the one nobody else can see.*
