## Spell: E.S.P.

The E.S.P. spell allows the caster to reach into the minds of others, sensing their thoughts, uncovering intentions and subtly influencing their perceptions. The depth of this mental intrusion depends upon the caster's magical proficiency and the strength of the subject's mind. Novices may perceive little more than fleeting emotions or surface thoughts, while experienced sorcerers can plant convincing illusions and suggestions that their victims mistake for their own ideas.

Throughout Cryptillia, E.S.P. has earned a reputation as one of the more unsettling forms of magic. Its applications range from uncovering deception and anticipating an adversary's intentions to manipulating unsuspecting individuals into making decisions they would otherwise avoid. A sufficiently skilled caster might convince an enemy that danger lies where there is none, or that an obviously foolish course of action is entirely sensible. Such influence can occasionally cause a victim to harm themselves, or inadvertently act in the caster's favour.

The spell is most effective against distracted, unsuspecting or weak-minded individuals. In the confusion of battle, maintaining the concentration required for mental manipulation becomes considerably more difficult, making E.S.P. better suited to quiet encounters, interrogation, deception and carefully planned schemes. Strong-willed subjects and those trained to recognise magical interference may resist its effects, while other spellcasters can prove particularly troublesome targets.

Unlike spells that command the elements or alter the physical world, E.S.P. leaves little visible evidence of its use. Its victims may never realise that their thoughts have been influenced, attributing their actions to poor judgement, sudden inspiration or simple misfortune.

Amongst Cryptillian scholars of the arcane, a troubling question is often raised: *If a thought can be placed inside your mind, how can you be certain it was ever yours?*
