## Spell: Open

The Open spell allows the caster to force open locked doors, sealed containers and other secured objects through a concentrated burst of magical energy. Rather than delicately manipulating a lock, the enchantment creates a small, controlled explosion within the locking mechanism or seal, violently breaking whatever holds the object shut. The force and effectiveness of the spell depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy expended. Novices may succeed in bursting a simple lock, while experienced sorcerers can tear heavy doors from their hinges or split reinforced chests apart.

Throughout Cryptillia, Open is valued by adventurers, explorers and, perhaps unsurprisingly, thieves. It provides a convenient means of gaining access to sealed chambers, forgotten tombs and treasure chests whose keys have long since disappeared. For those less concerned with lawful ownership, the spell offers a tempting alternative to conventional lockpicking, although its destructive nature makes it rather less discreet than a skilled thief might prefer.

Unlike the careful work of a locksmith, Open makes little effort to preserve the object being unlocked. Splintered doors, shattered hinges and ruined locking mechanisms are common consequences, while fragile contents may suffer considerably when their container is violently forced apart. The resulting noise can also attract unwanted attention, making the spell a questionable choice when stealth is essential.

The enchantment is not without its limitations. Particularly strong barriers, magically protected locks and exceptionally sturdy constructions may resist its effects, while careless casting in confined spaces can endanger anyone standing nearby. A treasure chest might yield its contents readily enough, but those contents are considerably less valuable if they have been scattered across the room in a shower of splinters.

Amongst Cryptillian thieves, an old saying captures both the appeal and the shortcomings of the spell: *Any fool can open a locked door. The clever ones do it without waking the guards.*
