## Spell: Invisible

The Invisible spell allows the caster to vanish from sight, concealing themselves and their immediate surroundings from ordinary vision. The duration and effectiveness of the enchantment depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy expended. Novices may struggle to maintain their concealment for long, while experienced sorcerers can remain unseen for extended periods, moving through the world without attracting so much as a passing glance.

Throughout Cryptillia, Invisible is prized by adventurers, spies, thieves and those who would rather avoid an unpleasant encounter altogether. It allows the caster to slip past guards, approach unsuspecting enemies or escape situations where confrontation would be unwise. In battle, an invisible opponent presents a formidable challenge, able to strike from unexpected directions while leaving their adversaries struggling to locate the source of the attack.

However, invisibility is not the same as being undetectable. The spell conceals the caster from sight but does nothing to silence their footsteps, suppress their scent or prevent them from disturbing their surroundings. Footprints in mud, ripples in water and the unmistakable sound of someone knocking over a suit of armour can all betray an otherwise perfectly concealed sorcerer. Creatures that rely upon hearing, smell or other senses may prove particularly difficult to deceive.

The enchantment also offers no physical protection. An invisible caster can still be struck by a sword, caught in a trap or injured by an attack directed towards their last known position. Other forms of magic may reveal or counteract the concealment, making overconfidence especially dangerous when dealing with experienced spellcasters.

Amongst Cryptillian thieves, a familiar warning is passed from master to apprentice: *Just because nobody can see you doesn't mean nobody knows you're there.*
