## Spell: Jump

The Jump spell allows the caster to propel themselves into the air in a powerful leap, travelling upwards and forwards in an arc far beyond the capabilities of ordinary human movement. The height and distance achieved depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy expended. Novices may find themselves clearing fences and small obstacles with ease, while experienced sorcerers can leap across ravines, scale formidable walls or soar over the heads of their enemies.

Throughout Cryptillia, Jump is regarded as a useful and relatively accessible alternative to the more demanding Fly spell. Adventurers employ it to cross dangerous terrain, evade ground-dwelling creatures and reach otherwise inaccessible locations. In combat, a well-timed leap can carry the caster clear of an approaching attack or allow them to descend upon an unsuspecting opponent from an unexpected direction.

Unlike Fly, which allows sustained movement through the air, Jump provides only a single burst of magical propulsion. Once airborne, the caster follows a natural trajectory, with limited opportunity to alter their course. This makes careful judgement essential, particularly when leaping across deep chasms or towards narrow ledges. The spell may provide the means to reach an extraordinary height, but it does not necessarily guarantee a comfortable landing.

Although Jump is commonly taught to inexperienced spellcasters, its apparent simplicity can encourage reckless experimentation. Misjudging a leap may result in an unfortunate collision with a ceiling, an unexpectedly distant landing or the discovery that the far side of a ravine is considerably further away than it first appeared.

Amongst Cryptillian adventurers, a familiar warning is often repeated: *Before you jump, make sure you know where you're going to land.*
