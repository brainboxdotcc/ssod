## Spell: Fire

The Fire spell allows the caster to conjure and manipulate flames, ranging from a small flicker at their fingertips to a roaring torrent of fire. The intensity, reach and duration of the flames depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy they are willing to expend. Novices may struggle to produce little more than a candle flame, while experienced sorcerers can unleash devastating streams of fire capable of engulfing their enemies.

Throughout Cryptillia, Fire serves purposes both practical and destructive. Travellers use it to light campfires, illuminate dark passages and provide warmth in freezing conditions. In battle, it becomes a formidable weapon, particularly against creatures vulnerable to heat and flame. However, conjured fire behaves much like ordinary fire, readily igniting anything combustible in its path.

The spell demands considerable care, especially in forests, crowded settlements and enclosed spaces. A moment's recklessness can turn a useful flame into an uncontrollable inferno, and the caster is no more immune to the resulting blaze than anyone else. Even the most accomplished sorcerer may find that starting a fire is considerably easier than putting one out.

Amongst Cryptillian apprentices, a familiar warning is often repeated: *Just because you can summon fire doesn't mean you can control what it burns.*
