## Spell: Fast Hands

The Fast Hands spell allows the caster to move their hands and arms at extraordinary speeds for brief periods, performing actions far faster than ordinary human reflexes would permit. The speed and duration of the enchantment depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy expended. While novices may achieve little more than unusually swift movements, experienced sorcerers can perform intricate actions in the blink of an eye.

Throughout Cryptillia, Fast Hands has applications both legitimate and dishonest. Craftsmen use it for repetitive tasks requiring exceptional speed, while warriors may employ it to draw weapons, strike rapidly or react to an opponent's movements. It is particularly notorious amongst gamblers, thieves and pickpockets, who use the spell to manipulate cards, exchange dice or relieve unsuspecting victims of their possessions before anyone notices.

The enchantment is notoriously difficult to detect through ordinary observation. To an untrained eye, a skilled caster's movements may appear instantaneous, leaving little evidence of magical interference. Other spellcasters, however, may recognise the telltale signs of the enchantment, making its use particularly risky in establishments accustomed to magical cheating.

Despite its advantages, Fast Hands does not grant the caster greater strength, endurance or protection from injury. Moving faster than the body can comfortably withstand may result in strained muscles, damaged joints or painful encounters with objects that prove considerably less willing to move out of the way.

Amongst Cryptillian gamblers, an old warning persists: *The hand may be quicker than the eye, but never assume the eye belongs to a fool.*
