## Spell: Bolt

The Bolt spell allows the caster to unleash a concentrated projectile of superheated plasma from their fingertips, striking enemies with a devastating combination of heat and explosive force. The power, range and intensity of the discharge depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy expended. Novices may produce little more than a brief, scorching flash, while experienced sorcerers can launch searing bolts capable of inflicting grievous injuries upon even formidable adversaries.

Throughout Cryptillia, Bolt is regarded as a dependable offensive spell, valued for its speed and directness. Unlike Fire, which produces sustained flames capable of spreading beyond their intended target, Bolt concentrates its destructive energy into a single discharge. This makes it particularly useful in combat, where a precisely aimed strike can incapacitate an opponent without setting the surrounding countryside ablaze.

Often described as the little brother of Thunderbolt, the spell shares its more powerful counterpart's reputation for spectacular destruction, although the two differ considerably in their nature. Bolt relies upon intensely heated plasma rather than a discharge of lightning, making its effects predominantly thermal rather than electrical. Its impact can scorch armour, burn flesh and ignite combustible materials, while the sudden release of energy may cause additional damage at the point of contact.

Despite its apparent simplicity, Bolt demands careful aim and an awareness of one's surroundings. A missed shot may strike something considerably less deserving of destruction, and the spell's concentrated heat makes it especially hazardous in confined spaces or near flammable materials.

Amongst Cryptillian battlemages, a familiar piece of advice is offered to eager apprentices: *Point first, cast second. The order is important.*
