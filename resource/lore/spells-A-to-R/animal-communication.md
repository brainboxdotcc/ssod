## Spell: Animal Communication

The Animal Communication spell allows the caster to establish a telepathic connection with animals, exchanging thoughts, emotions and intentions without the need for spoken language. The clarity and depth of this communication depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the nature of the creature involved. Novices may perceive little more than simple feelings of hunger, fear or curiosity, while experienced sorcerers can hold surprisingly meaningful exchanges with creatures whose minds are receptive to their influence.

Throughout Cryptillia, Animal Communication has proven invaluable to travellers, hunters and those who make their homes in the wilderness. A passing bird might reveal the presence of danger ahead, a horse may communicate its distress, or a woodland creature might provide clues about something it has witnessed. However, animals perceive the world very differently from humans, and their understanding of events is shaped by instinct, experience and immediate concerns rather than abstract reasoning.

Unlike E.S.P., which is designed to explore and influence the minds of sentient beings, Animal Communication bridges the considerable differences between human and animal thought. It does not grant animals human intelligence, nor does it compel them to obey the caster's wishes. A wolf may understand a request perfectly well and still decide that the sorcerer looks considerably more appetising than whatever reward has been offered.

The spell demands patience and an appreciation of the creature's natural behaviour. Some animals are curious and cooperative, while others are suspicious, territorial or simply uninterested in conversation. Even a successful exchange can produce unexpected results when the caster and the animal have very different ideas about what constitutes useful information.

Amongst Cryptillian druids and wilderness travellers, a familiar saying offers a reminder of the spell's limitations: *Just because an animal can understand you doesn't mean it has anything worth saying.*
