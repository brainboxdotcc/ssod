## Spell: Light

The Light spell allows the caster to conjure a brilliant orb of magical illumination that hovers above their head, casting a steady glow comparable to that of a well-lit lantern. The brightness and reach of the light depend upon the caster's magical proficiency, with novices producing a modest glow and experienced sorcerers capable of illuminating large chambers and extensive stretches of darkness.

Throughout Cryptillia, Light is amongst the most practical and widely used spells, particularly by adventurers, miners and travellers who frequently venture into places untouched by daylight. Unlike torches and oil lamps, the magical orb requires no fuel, produces no smoke and cannot be extinguished by wind, rain or immersion in water. Its steady radiance makes it invaluable when exploring ancient ruins, navigating underground passages or travelling through unfamiliar wilderness after nightfall.

Once summoned, the orb remains above the caster, following their movements without requiring constant attention. The enchantment persists until the caster deliberately dismisses it or is defeated, at which point the light immediately vanishes. This remarkable endurance makes Light especially useful during lengthy expeditions, although its reliability can encourage adventurers to neglect more conventional sources of illumination.

The spell does have its disadvantages. A glowing orb above one's head is difficult to conceal, making the caster conspicuous to enemies, predators and anything else lurking in the darkness. Its light may reveal hidden dangers, but it can just as readily announce the arrival of an unsuspecting traveller to creatures that would otherwise have remained unaware of their presence.

Amongst Cryptillian adventurers, a familiar warning is often repeated: *A light may help you see what lies ahead, but it also helps whatever lies ahead see you.*
