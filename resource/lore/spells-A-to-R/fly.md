## Spell: Fly

The Fly spell grants the caster the ability to rise into the air and travel freely above the ground, unhindered by the limitations of ordinary movement. The height, speed and duration of flight depend upon the caster's magical proficiency and the energy they can sustain. Experienced sorcerers may soar above forests, mountains and castle walls, while novices often find themselves struggling to rise much higher than the nearest rooftop.

Flight has obvious advantages throughout Cryptillia, allowing adventurers to cross otherwise impassable terrain, escape dangerous creatures and survey the land from above. However, the spell provides no protection against strong winds, freezing temperatures or the attentions of creatures that naturally inhabit the skies.

Unlike many enchantments, Fly demands particular attention to its duration. When the magic expires, the caster is immediately subject to gravity, regardless of their altitude. More than one overconfident sorcerer has discovered, rather too late, that reaching a great height is considerably easier than returning safely from it.

Amongst experienced spellcasters, a simple piece of advice is passed down to every apprentice: *Never fly higher than you're prepared to fall.*
