## Spell: Grow

The Grow spell allows the caster to increase the size of themselves, another living creature, or an inanimate object to proportions limited by their magical ability and the energy expended. Like its counterpart, Shrink, the spell alters only physical dimensions, leaving the subject's other properties unchanged.

When cast upon living creatures, the effects are temporary, eventually wearing off and restoring the subject to their natural size. Inanimate objects, however, retain their enlarged dimensions indefinitely unless restored through other magical means.

Grow has numerous practical applications throughout Cryptillia. Craftsmen use it to enlarge delicate mechanisms for inspection and repair, while builders and labourers may employ it to produce oversized tools or structures. In combat, an enlarged warrior can become an imposing adversary, although greater size does not necessarily confer greater skill or invulnerability.

The spell demands considerable caution, particularly when used indoors or near other people. An object enlarged beyond the confines of its surroundings can cause catastrophic structural damage, while an excessively ambitious casting may transform a modest workshop into the centre of a very large and expensive disaster.

Amongst Cryptillian craftsmen, the spell has inspired a cautionary saying: *Before you grow it, make sure there's room for it.*
