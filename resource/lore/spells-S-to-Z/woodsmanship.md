## Spell: Woodsmanship

The Woodsmanship spell temporarily grants the caster the knowledge and instincts of an experienced woodsman, allowing them to navigate and survive in the wilderness with remarkable confidence. For an entire day, they gain an intuitive understanding of woodland terrain, animal tracks, edible plants and the many subtle signs that reveal the secrets of a forest.

Unlike enchantments that simply enhance an existing ability, Woodsmanship imparts practical knowledge that complements the caster's natural skills. An inexperienced traveller may suddenly recognise a safe route through dense undergrowth, identify signs of nearby wildlife or locate suitable materials for shelter. Those already familiar with the wilderness find their abilities sharpened further.

Such an extensive transfer of knowledge places considerable demands upon the caster's magic. The spell can only be invoked once every seven days, making careful timing essential. An adventurer who squanders its benefits on an afternoon stroll may find themselves considerably less prepared when facing a genuinely dangerous wilderness several days later.
