## Spell: Shrink

The Shrink spell allows the caster to reduce the size of themselves, another living creature, or an inanimate object to a fraction of its original dimensions. The degree of reduction depends upon the caster's will and the magical energy expended. The spell alters only physical size, leaving the subject's other properties unchanged.

When cast upon living creatures, the effects are temporary, eventually wearing off and restoring the subject to their natural size. Inanimate objects, however, remain diminished indefinitely unless restored through other magical means.

Despite being one of the simpler spells taught to young apprentices, Shrink has found remarkable uses throughout Cryptillia. Craftsmen employ it to produce intricate mechanical devices, constructing elaborate mechanisms at a manageable scale before reducing them to sizes otherwise impossible to manufacture. Pocket watches, precision instruments and miniature clockwork contraptions owe much of their existence to this humble enchantment.

Nevertheless, the spell is not without its dangers. A shrunken adventurer may find an ordinary house cat a formidable predator, while careless application to delicate machinery can render months of painstaking craftsmanship too small to locate, let alone operate. Excessive magical enthusiasm has been responsible for more than one expensive disappearance.

As the old saying amongst Cryptillian craftsmen goes: *Measure twice, shrink once.*
