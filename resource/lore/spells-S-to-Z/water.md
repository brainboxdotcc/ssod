## Spell: Water

The Water spell allows the caster to conjure a powerful torrent of fresh water from almost any part of their body, most commonly the palms of their hands. Some sorcerers prefer more theatrical displays, producing streams from their mouths or even their eyes, although such extravagance offers little practical advantage beyond startling onlookers.

In combat, the sudden rush of water can knock opponents off balance, force them backwards or sweep them into hazardous positions. While less immediately destructive than Fire or Thunderbolt, its ability to disrupt an enemy's footing makes it surprisingly effective when used with imagination.

Beyond battle, Water is one of the most useful survival spells an adventurer can possess. A reliable supply of fresh drinking water can mean the difference between life and death when crossing the scorching deserts of Cryptillia. It can also extinguish fires, fill empty containers or wash away accumulated filth, proving that even the simplest necessities can become extraordinary when summoned by magic.
