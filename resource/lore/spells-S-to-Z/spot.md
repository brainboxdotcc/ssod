## Spell: Spot

Not every treasure is left in plain sight, nor every passage marked by an obvious doorway. The Spot spell sharpens the caster's awareness of their surroundings, revealing concealed valuables, hidden exits and other secrets that might otherwise escape even the most careful examination. A brief incantation is all that is required, making it one of the quickest and most convenient enchantments available to an inquisitive adventurer.

The spell is particularly useful in ancient ruins, forgotten tombs and labyrinthine dungeons, where secret compartments and disguised entrances may have remained undiscovered for centuries. A concealed door might reveal itself in an apparently solid wall, while a valuable object overlooked amongst ordinary possessions may suddenly become apparent. Such discoveries can mean the difference between leaving a dungeon empty-handed and emerging with a fortune.

Spot does not create treasures or passages where none exist, nor does it explain how to reach or open everything it reveals. A hidden doorway may still be locked, obstructed or protected by traps, and discovering a valuable object does not necessarily make it safe to retrieve. The spell reveals opportunities, but leaves the practical difficulties of exploiting them to the caster.

Its usefulness in combat is limited. Although the incantation can be performed quickly, revealing concealed treasures and exits offers little advantage against an enemy already swinging a sword. A resourceful adventurer might discover an unexpected escape route, but Spot is primarily a tool of exploration rather than battle.

There is also a certain danger in becoming too dependent upon magical assistance. Adventurers who rely entirely upon Spot may neglect the ordinary skills of observation, overlooking clues that a patient explorer would recognise without expending a drop of mana.

After all, discovering a secret door is only half the adventure. The other half is deciding whether opening it is a good idea.
