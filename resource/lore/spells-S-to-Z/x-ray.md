## Spell: X-Ray

The X-Ray spell grants the caster the extraordinary ability to see through solid objects, revealing what lies hidden beyond walls, doors and other seemingly impenetrable barriers. Unlike its scientific namesake, this magical vision is not obstructed by dense materials such as lead, allowing the sorcerer to peer through almost any physical obstruction.

For explorers and treasure hunters, the possibilities are considerable. Concealed chambers, the contents of locked chests and dangers lurking beyond closed doors can all be examined without disturbing their surroundings. A cautious adventurer might use the spell to investigate a suspicious passage before entering, potentially avoiding an unpleasant encounter with whatever awaits on the other side.

However, X-Ray requires considerable time and concentration to cast, making it unsuitable for combat or situations demanding an immediate response. Its greatest value lies in careful exploration and preparation, where knowing what lies ahead can be far more useful than discovering it the hard way.
