## Spell: Thunderbolt

Among the most destructive offensive spells known to sorcery, Thunderbolt summons a tremendous charge of electrical energy around the caster's arms. Within moments, crackling arcs of lightning dance across their fingertips, ready to be unleashed towards a chosen target. The resulting discharge strikes with devastating force, capable of inflicting terrible injuries or reducing an unfortunate victim to smouldering ashes.

Unlike its lesser cousin, Bolt, which hurls concentrated plasma, Thunderbolt channels the raw power of electricity. Its effects are equally formidable against living creatures and inanimate objects, making it useful for shattering obstacles as well as overwhelming powerful adversaries.

Such destructive power demands careful aim. Lightning is notoriously unforgiving, and an ill-judged discharge can leave considerably more devastation than the caster intended. Thunderbolt is therefore a spell best reserved for situations where overwhelming force is not merely desirable, but necessary.
