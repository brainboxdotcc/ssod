## Spell: Weapon Skill

The Weapon Skill spell temporarily transforms the caster into a warrior of extraordinary ability, quadrupling their natural combat skill for a short period. Movements become precise, reactions sharpen, and even an inexperienced swordsman may suddenly wield their weapon with the confidence and finesse of a seasoned veteran. The enchantment lasts only briefly, but can decisively turn the tide of a difficult battle.

Such remarkable prowess comes at a price. Once the spell expires, the caster's combat skill falls to half its normal effectiveness for a comparable period. Blows become less accurate, defensive movements falter, and opponents who were easily outmanoeuvred moments earlier may suddenly gain the upper hand.

Repeated castings compound this penalty, making the spell increasingly dangerous to rely upon. A wise sorcerer therefore uses Weapon Skill to finish a confrontation quickly, rather than prolonging a battle they may be poorly equipped to survive once the magic fades.
