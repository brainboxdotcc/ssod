## Spell: Strength

The Strength spell grants the caster a momentary surge of extraordinary physical power, allowing them to perform feats far beyond the capabilities of an ordinary mortal. For a fleeting instant, muscles become imbued with magical energy, transforming an otherwise unremarkable movement into an act of tremendous force.

Most commonly employed in combat, the enchantment allows a sorcerer to deliver a devastating blow capable of sending an opponent sprawling. A punch that would ordinarily cause little more than a bruised jaw might instead hurl an adversary backwards, while a weapon swung under its influence can strike with terrifying power. Even a physically weak spellcaster can become a formidable threat when the enchantment is timed correctly.

The spell's usefulness extends beyond fighting. A sudden burst of strength might allow an adventurer to force a stubborn door, shift a heavy obstruction or break free from a physical restraint. However, the magic lasts for such a short time that the intended action must be carefully prepared. It is of little use for carrying heavy loads over long distances or performing sustained physical labour.

There is also the matter of controlling such unnatural force. Strength does not necessarily grant the caster greater durability, balance or resistance to injury. Striking a solid stone wall with magically enhanced power may prove considerably more damaging to the caster's hand than to the wall itself. Likewise, attempting to lift an enormous object without proper footing can result in an embarrassing, and potentially painful, loss of balance.

Experienced practitioners learn that the secret of Strength lies not in how much power they can summon, but in knowing precisely when and where to apply it. A single well-placed blow can settle a confrontation before an opponent has time to react.

Of course, there is a considerable difference between having the strength to throw a giant across a room and having the wisdom not to attempt it.
