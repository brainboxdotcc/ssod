## Spell: Sneak

The Sneak spell enhances the caster's ability to move unnoticed, tripling their natural stealth for a limited time. Footsteps become quieter, movements more deliberate, and the caster seems almost instinctively drawn towards shadows and places of concealment. Unlike Invisible, the spell does not hide the caster from sight, but instead makes them extraordinarily difficult to detect through ordinary observation.

For thieves, scouts and adventurers hoping to avoid unwanted attention, Sneak can be invaluable. A guarded corridor becomes easier to traverse, a sleeping beast less likely to awaken, and an unsuspecting enemy considerably easier to approach. However, the enchantment cannot conceal someone standing openly in plain sight, nor can it prevent careless actions from betraying their presence.

The spell's greatest drawback becomes apparent when its effects expire. The magical enhancement leaves the caster temporarily clumsy and conspicuous, reducing their natural stealth to half its usual effectiveness during the ensuing recovery period. Footsteps that were previously silent may suddenly seem thunderous, and movements once performed effortlessly become awkward and poorly timed.

Repeated castings compound this penalty, making prolonged reliance upon Sneak increasingly hazardous. An overconfident thief might slip unnoticed through an entire castle, only to discover that escaping with the stolen treasure is considerably more difficult when every creaking floorboard seems determined to announce their departure.

Experienced practitioners therefore plan not only how to enter a dangerous place, but also how to leave it before their borrowed subtlety deserts them.
