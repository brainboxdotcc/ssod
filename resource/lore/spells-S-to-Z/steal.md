## Spell: Steal

The Steal spell is a specialised enchantment designed for those moments when a light touch and a little magical assistance can make all the difference. By temporarily doubling the caster's sneak score, it grants a brief but significant advantage when attempting to relieve an unsuspecting individual of their possessions. The enchantment is particularly suited to pickpockets, opportunistic thieves and adventurers whose curiosity occasionally extends to the contents of other people's pockets.

Unlike the more sustained effects of Sneak, Steal provides only a fleeting opportunity. Its magic is concentrated into a short burst of heightened stealth, allowing the caster to make a carefully timed attempt at theft before the enhancement fades. Success still depends upon the circumstances, the alertness of the intended victim and the caster's own skill. Magic can make a hand harder to notice, but it cannot guarantee that the owner of a stolen purse will remain oblivious.

The spell is also unusually demanding to prepare. Once invoked, its intricate magical workings require a lengthy recovery before it can be cast again. This makes repeated attempts impractical and encourages thieves to choose their targets carefully. A failed attempt may leave the caster without magical assistance precisely when it would be most useful.

Its lengthy preparation also makes Steal unsuitable for combat. An enemy actively trying to remove the caster's head is unlikely to provide the uninterrupted concentration necessary to complete the enchantment, and the spell offers little benefit in a direct confrontation.

The most accomplished thieves understand that Steal is not a substitute for patience, timing or a convincing distraction. Indeed, the greatest challenge is often not taking something unnoticed, but behaving naturally afterwards.

A stolen purse is of little use if its former owner catches you admiring the contents.
