## Spell: Shield

The Shield spell conjures a protective bubble of magical energy around the caster and any companions standing close enough to share its protection. The barrier provides an armour rating of 6, helping to deflect weapons, projectiles and other physical attacks. Although invisible until struck, the shield may briefly shimmer or ripple as it absorbs the force of an incoming blow.

Unlike many defensive enchantments, Shield cannot be cast in the heat of battle. Forming the barrier requires time, concentration and an uninterrupted opportunity to complete the incantation. A sorcerer who waits until an enemy is charging towards them will almost certainly find themselves struck before the spell can take effect.

For this reason, experienced adventurers tend to cast Shield before entering dangerous territory, anticipating an ambush or confronting a known threat. Its protection extends to nearby allies, making it particularly valuable to travelling parties whose members may lack adequate armour of their own.

The barrier is neither permanent nor impenetrable. Its magic eventually dissipates, and sufficiently powerful attacks can still injure those sheltering within it. Nor does it prevent an enemy from simply waiting for the enchantment to expire.

A well-prepared sorcerer may enter battle behind a formidable magical defence. An unprepared one may discover that several minutes of careful spellcasting are difficult to accomplish with an axe embedded in their shoulder.
