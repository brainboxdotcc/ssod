The First Age of Magic is a period of early Cryptillian history marked by centuries of magical warfare. Much of what is known about the age survives through incomplete records, later accounts and legend, but there is little doubt that magic was used on a scale rarely seen since.

At the time, the peoples of Cryptillia understood considerably less about the dangers of magic. Spells were cast freely and increasingly powerful forms of magic were developed as each side sought an advantage over its enemies. Most were also unaware of the demonic forces which existed beyond their world, or of the attention such widespread use of magic was attracting.

After centuries of conflict, wizards from several of the warring races finally began working together. Humans, Elves and Dwarves who had once used their magic against one another instead sought a way to end the wars permanently.

Their answer was the Peace Pact.

Its articles were written upon magical scrolls created to be indestructible. The intention was that the Pact would bind the races to a lasting peace which no future ruler could simply tear up, burn or conveniently consign to history.

Something went badly wrong.

Not every delegation reached the peace summit. The Orc Empire was absent, as were several Elven tribes whose descendants would eventually become known as the Dark Elves. The disappearance of the Elven delegation remains particularly important to Dark Elf history, remembered by them as the Severance.

Demonic interference is widely believed to have been responsible, although after so much time it is difficult to separate established history from later interpretation. Whatever happened, the missing delegations meant that the intended gathering of Cryptillia's peoples was already incomplete.

Worse was to follow.

Before the Peace Pact could be properly ratified, its supposedly indestructible scrolls were corrupted. The magic intended to preserve them remained, but their purpose was twisted. The articles of peace became the Seven Spells of Destruction.

The treaty intended to end centuries of magical warfare had instead produced seven magical objects which could not be destroyed and whose power threatened every race in Cryptillia.

The First Age of Magic ended, but not with the eternal peace its survivors had intended. The wars had demonstrated the terrible power of unrestricted magic, revealed the existence and influence of demonic forces, and left Cryptillia with the Seven Spells.

The peoples of Cryptillia had learned that magic was considerably more dangerous than they had believed.

They had learned it rather late.
