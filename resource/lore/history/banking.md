Across Cryptillia, banking is handled by a network of secure establishments known as Guildbanks. Behind their guarded doors and heavily fortified vaults, they hold the gold and valuables of anyone sensible enough not to carry their entire fortune around with them.

Opening an account at a Guildbank gives you a place within those vaults. Speak to a teller and they will accept your gold or valuables for safekeeping. When the account is opened, the Guildbank records your face using identification magic. From then on, you are your own account identifier.

The Guildbanks are connected, so you need not return to the same branch to reclaim your possessions. Visit any Guildbank and a teller can use the same magic to recognise you, locate your account and arrange for your gold or belongings to be brought from another vault by secure teleportation magic. There are no documents to carry and no account numbers to remember; if you have somehow misplaced your face, banking is probably the least of your concerns.

For those with considerably more gold than you, Guildbanks offer other services. Loans can be arranged, currencies exchanged, and money invested in ventures across Cryptillia. Fortunes have undoubtedly been made this way. Fortunes have also undoubtedly been lost. At present, your own finances are unlikely to trouble the Guildbank's investment department.

Naturally, keeping large quantities of other people's gold in one place attracts attention. Guildbank vaults are protected by magical wards, skilled guards and formidable locks, with further precautions which the Guildbanks keep to themselves. Few thieves are foolish enough to test them, and fewer still are known to have succeeded.

For most adventurers, that is reason enough to use one. Whatever dangers await you beyond the town gates, at least you needn't take your savings with you.
